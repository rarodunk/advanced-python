Local knowledge

    Geography is deliberately exaggerated — the mountains are fatter and the motu bigger than life so everything is tappable, but your own position is projected onto it from real coordinates, so the blue dot really is where you are. Hours and prices on a 15,000-person island change with the weather and the family calendar; ring ahead or ask at your accommodation. Travel times assume the 32 km ring road at 35 km/h on a scooter and 4.5 km/h on foot. Directions hand your maps app a position derived from this map's own geometry — good to a few hundred metres, which finds the right stretch of road rather than the driveway. On an island with no street addresses, that is how everyone navigates anyway.
